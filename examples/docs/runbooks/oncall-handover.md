# On-call handover

The handover happens every Monday at 10:00 in a 15-minute call.

## What the outgoing engineer covers

- Open incidents and their current status
- Alerts that fired more than twice in the week and whether they need tuning
- Any change freeze or planned maintenance in the coming week

## What the incoming engineer checks

- They can log in to the paging tool and receive a test page
- They have read access to the dashboards and the runbook repository

## Handover notes

Write the notes in the team channel using the headings above, so the next person can
find them.
